# 🍃 Focus Mint

> An immersive, highly-polished motivational study dashboard and productivity companion designed to cultivate a calm, structured, and deeply focused mind.

Focus Mint merges relaxing visual aesthetics, atmospheric soundscapes, dynamic weather effects, and high-fidelity productivity utilities into a single cohesive desktop environment.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌌 1. Dynamic Atmosphere & Interactive Themes
*   **True Parallax 3D Canvas:** Custom-optimized particle drift grids, slow-hovering cozy focus lines, and visual depths responding smoothly to cursor coordinates.
*   **Weather Particle Overlays:** Fluid, high-performance CSS animation overlays rendering rain droplets on wet Monstera leaves, gentle winter snow flurries, or glowing summer sun-motes depending on your selection.
*   **Curated Environment Presets:**
    *   **Cozy Desk Greenery:** Warm, golden greenhouse lighting paired with lush indoor plants.
    *   **Mind Zen Pathway:** Cool, meditative mountain forest vibes surrounded by mystical digital flora.

### 🎵 2. Synthesized Audio Controller ("The Device")
*   **Multi-Source Audio Deck:** An integrated retro audio dashboard with interactive volume controls, progress visualizations, and track labels.
*   **Ambient Synthetics:** Crackling log fires, soothing rainstorms, ticking clocks, or cozy ambient chimes and bells.
*   **Lo-Fi Chill Beats:** Seamlessly toggle procedural looping tracks designed to maintain optimal alpha-wave focus.

### ⏱️ 3. High-Fidelity Productivity Utilities
*   **Concentration Focus Clock:** Customizable Pomodoro timer accompanied by status chimes, responsive start/pause triggers, and hydration/break reminders.
*   **Dynamic Task Checklist:** Real-time action notebooks styled as hand-drawn paper sheets to plan your priorities, commit adjustments, and check off accomplishments.
*   **Color-Coded Sticky Notes:** Jot down digital sticky boards (utilizing Amber, Sky, Pink, and Emerald palettes) for spontaneous ideas or temporary notes.

### 🧠 4. Mind Gym & Cognitive Training
Unwind or calibrate your brain with built-in, completely client-side interactive board/card games:
*   **Sudoku:** A custom-styled matrix puzzle generator with interactive numpad selections and instant error validations.
*   **Chess Simulator:** A pristine, lightweight physical chess engine for strategic calibration against yourself or friends.
*   **Discipline Quiz:** Test your knowledge and habits on daily mindfulness, focus, and general productivity heuristics.
*   **Memory Match:** Classic visual card-flipping test to sharpen short-term retention.

### 📚 5. Focus Wisdom Portal
Access curated, actionable tactical blueprints drawn from top discipline literature:
*   *Atomic Habits* by James Clear
*   *Deep Work* by Cal Newport
*   *Mindset* by Carol S. Dweck
*   *The 5 AM Club* by Robin Sharma

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

Focus Mint is constructed as a lightweight, zero-bloat Single Page Application (SPA):

*   **Runtime & Compiler:** React 18, TypeScript, Vite
*   **3D Presentation Layer:** Three.js
*   **Animation Engine:** `motion/react` + GSAP Transitions
*   **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (configured natively with fine-tuned shadow classes and subtle micro-layouts)
*   **Iconography:** `lucide-react`
